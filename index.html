Circuit Field Guide

Tabletop Circuit 2027

Major Warhammer 40,000, Age of Sigmar, Kill Team, and X-Wing tournaments across the Games Workshop Warhammer Open series, Green Banner Event Co., Frontline Gaming, AdeptiCon, the American Team Championship, and the World Team Championship. Starts with what's left of the 2026 season, then runs through 2027. Nothing for 2027 is announced yet outside three confirmed stops — every other 2027 date below is projected from its 2026 running, advanced 364 days to land on the same weekend.

Formats

Methodology — the four events running before January 2027 are this year's actual, already-announced 2026 dates. For everything after, where the organizer hasn't announced 2027 dates, each event's 2026 start/end date is advanced by 364 days (52 weeks), which preserves the day of week and lands on the same weekend the event ran in 2026. This is a planning estimate, not an official date — always confirm against the organizer before booking travel.

Format tags — each event's Format tags (40K GT, 40K Narrative, 40K Team, AOS, Kill Team, X-Wing) reflect the parallel tournament tracks published for its 2026 running, at the same venue and weekend — they aren't separate dates. "X-Wing (TMG)" is AdeptiCon's Star Wars: X-Wing — The Miniatures Game track, run there by the community-led X-Wing Alliance (XWA), which held its first World Championship at AdeptiCon 2026; whether XWA returns for 2027 isn't yet confirmed.

Calendar buttons — "+ Google Calendar" opens a pre-filled event ready to save. Proton Calendar has no equivalent one-click link, so "Copy for Proton Calendar" instead copies the event as .ics text — paste it into a text file, save it with a .ics extension, then import it via Proton Calendar → Settings → Import/export.

My Events — click ☆ on any card to star it; starred events show a ★ on the timeline and can be isolated with the "My Events only" toggle. This is saved in this browser's local storage, so it's private to this device and this browser — it isn't synced anywhere, and clearing site data or opening the page elsewhere resets it.

Reserved — each card has four icons (flight, rental car, hotel, event ticket) to click once you've booked that piece — they go from a faint outline to solid once marked. Saved the same way as My Events, in this browser's local storage only.

Conflicts — when two of your ★ starred events overlap in date, each shows a red "Overlaps…" banner naming the other, and the timeline draws a red band across the overlapping days. This only fires between events you've starred — different circuits sharing a weekend happens constantly and isn't flagged.

Your own events — "+ Add your own event" adds anything this list doesn't cover — a side event, travel, a personal note — into the timeline and list like any other entry, filterable under the "My Custom Events" circuit. Saved only in this browser's local storage; "Remove event" on the card deletes it for good.

Golden Ticket — a Golden Ticket is Games Workshop's own qualification invite to the World Championships of Warhammer, earned by placing well at a sanctioned event. Every Frontline Gaming event here is marked GW-eligible on its own ticket page; every GW Warhammer Open stop and AdeptiCon and the ATC are confirmed via GW's own "ways to qualify" articles. Green Banner's own "Gold Ticket" is a VIP merch tier, not a GW qualifier, and is left unmarked; the WTC's Golden Tickets are awarded through a separate regional WTC Qualifier event, not the WTC finals itself. Confirm eligibility on the organizer's page before counting on it.
